## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tension, investigative editorial aesthetic tailored for deep-dive scrollytelling journalism. It translates the gravity of high-stakes criminal investigations into an immersive digital medium, balancing forensic precision with narrative drama.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes the sensation of examining unredacted legal dossiers inside a tactical operations briefing room. It commands intellectual focus, solemnity, and ethical weight. Visual elements must never feel casual, playful, or commercial; they project forensic rigor, evidentiary proof, and unyielding narrative pacing.

### Design Movement: Noir Investigative Editorial
The visual direction fuses **Editorial Typography** (dramatic contrast, traditional broadside serif titling, drop caps, multi-column reportage) with **Technical Forensic Brutalism** (monospaced telemetry, tactical coordinate stamps, wire-frame evidence frames, razor-thin structural borders). The interplay between textured document surfaces and stark dark-noir intelligence layers creates suspense during long-form scrollytelling sequences.

## Colors

The palette operates across dual narrative realities: the **Paper/Dossier Mode** (light canvas for courtroom arguments, background narrative, and official records) and the **Noir Evidence Mode** (deep tactical darks for sting operations, illicit supply chains, and interactive maps).

### Functional Color Applications
- **Primary Accent (`#7A1F1F` - Maroon / Deep Crimson):** Reserved strictly for major headlines, case titles, key legal indictments, and narrative climaxes. It delivers an authoritative, stark, and visceral tone without the artificial glare of bright digital red.
- **Secondary (`#1F2A44` - Deep Navy Blue):** Applied to secondary headers, thematic chapter markers, investigator log titles, and primary analytical frames.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#1B1B1F` - Charcoal):** The bedrock of reading typography on light surfaces, ensuring maximum optical density and reading endurance without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Canvas Systems:** 
  - *Light Canvas:* `#F8FAFC` base with `#FFFFFF` cards and documents.
  - *Noir Canvas:* `#0B0F19` deep obsidian with `#111827` dossier panels.
- **Evidence Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Used strictly as a tactical data accent for wiretaps, flight paths, vessel coordinates, timeline timestamps, and interactive pinpoints.
- **Alert & Risk Indicators:** `#DC2626` (Crimson Danger) for lethal infractions, high-risk targets, and seizures; `#F59E0B` (Signal Amber) for pending legal motions, surveillance flags, and intermediate warnings.
- **Borders & Rules:** `#374151` on dark frames; `#E2E8F0` and `#9CA3AF` on editorial records.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension through intentional friction between the dramatic, literary serif (`Playfair Display`), the neutral structural grotesque (`Inter`), and the clinical monospaced instrument (`JetBrains Mono`).

### Application Guidelines
- **Drop Caps:** Narrative chapters open with a single, dramatic 3-line drop cap set in `Playfair Display` Bold in `#7A1F1F`. The cap aligns precisely with the cap height of the first line and baseline of the third body line.
- **Editorial Sub-titles:** Secondary narrative titles utilize `#1F2A44` to delineate journalistic transitions, while standard headers preserve editorial legibility.
- **Evidence Telemetry:** Timestamps, case archive references (`VN10-CRIM-DOC`), geographic coordinates, seizure tallies, and cross-examination excerpts must be set strictly in `JetBrains Mono` to emphasize objective data over narrative emotion.
- **Vietnamese Diacritics:** Line heights are calibrated generously (`1.75` for body text, `1.2` for display) to prevent diacritic clipping on Vietnamese glyph clusters (e.g., `ệ`, `ẩ`, `ở`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric 12-column grid system optimized for editorial scrollytelling, interactive dossier sidecars, and cinematic full-viewport transitions.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 48px margins, 24px gutters. Long-form prose stays constrained to a single column spanning columns 4 through 9 (max-width 680px) to maintain optimal reading cadence (65-75 characters per line). Sticky data panels, interactive route maps, and suspect profiles occupy columns 1-3 or 10-12.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 16px gutters. Telemetry cards collapse into top or bottom pinned accordion rails.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px margins, 12px gutters. Pinned scrollytelling triggers become full-width bottom sheets or inline visual interruptions.

### Scrollytelling Mechanics
The reading experience relies on rhythmic spacing transitions:
- Narrative sections alternate between dense, multi-column document grids and expansive `space-xl` visual pauses.
- Interactive timeline checkpoints trigger full-bleed viewport pins with sticky background transitions (`#F8FAFC` daylight records transitioning smoothly into `#0B0F19` midnight surveillance operations).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects conventional software drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Dossier Layering** and **Razor Hairline Boundaries**, mirroring physical crime files and terminal readouts.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Primary read-stream (`#F8FAFC` or `#0B0F19`). Completely flat.
2. **Document & Evidence Slips (Level 1):** Forensic cards, quote pullouts, and tabular data containers use a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0` on light; `#374151` on dark) with an ultra-subtle, directional paper offset shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05), 0 10px 15px -5px rgba(27, 27, 31, 0.04)`.
3. **Surveillance & Modal Overlay (Level 2):** Tactical map overlays, pinned evidence sidebars, and wiretap transcripts employ a dark glass treatment: `#0B0F19` at 85% opacity with a `12px` backdrop blur and a `1px` crisp border in `#374151`.
4. **Target Stamp & Redaction Badges (Level 3):** High-priority alerts (`#DC2626`), seized asset totals, and suspect dossier markers float above content with zero blur, utilizing strict 1px solid high-contrast borders to evoke rubber ink stamps.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**. Every component, card, button, tag, and modal features crisp 90-degree right angles (`0px` border-radius).

### Design Intent
Zero roundedness reinforces legal certainty, structural tension, and technical utility. Rounded corners diminish the gravity of judicial and investigative findings. Beveled or pill shapes are forbidden; all containers must emulate guillotine-cut paper files, classified file folders, technical ledger grids, and terminal screens.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Action (Proceed / Inspect Archive):** Solid `#7A1F1F` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0px` radius, padding `12px 24px`. Font: `JetBrains Mono` 12px uppercase, tracking `0.1em`. Hover state: Inverts to `#FFFFFF` background with `#7A1F1F` 1px border and text.
- **Secondary Action (Filter Evidence / Toggle Map):** Transparent background, 1px solid `#1F2A44`, text `#1F2A44`. On noir backgrounds: 1px solid `#06B6D4`, text `#06B6D4`. Hover state: background fill at 10% opacity.
- **Tactile Sound/Scrolly Control:** A square icon button (44x44px) enclosed in a 1px border with a technical center glyph.

### Evidence Chips & Dossier Tags
- Set in `JetBrains Mono` 10px, uppercase, tracking `0.15em`.
- **Status "SEIZED":** Solid `#DC2626` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 2px 8px padding.
- **Status "MONITORED":** `#06B6D4` 1px outline, `#06B6D4` text, no fill.
- **Status "CLASSIFIED":** `#F59E0B` fill, `#1B1B1F` text.

### Tabular Data & Seizure Metrics
- Table headers: `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase in `#1F2A44`, bordered by a double-line rule (`border-bottom: 3px double #374151`).
- Numbers, weights (kg/tons), currencies (USD/VND), and vehicle registration numbers align strictly right with monospace tabbed figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- Alternating row fills: subtle `#F1F5F9` on light; `#111827` on noir.

### Interactive Scrollytelling Cards
- **Suspect & Location Cards:** Contained within a razor-thin border (`#E2E8F0` or `#374151`). Features a square portrait slot with desaturated imagery, overlaid by a technical coordinate header (`21°01'49.0"N 105°51'08.4"E`) and an accent status bar on the left edge (3px solid `#7A1F1F`).
- **Wiretap Audio Log:** A terminal-styled blockquote with a dark `#111827` background, glowing dynamic waveform in `#06B6D4`, and mono transcripts prefaced by precise millisecond timestamps (`00:14:28.102 [INTERCEPT]`).

### Form Inputs & Filters (Search the Case Files)
- Flat rectangular input field, zero border-radius.
- Default border: 1px solid `#9CA3AF`; active focus border: 1.5px solid `#7A1F1F` with no external glow ring.
- Placeholder text: `JetBrains Mono` 13px italicized `#9CA3AF`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Binary square toggles (16x16px), zero radius.
- Checked state: solid `#7A1F1F` with a crisp `#FFFFFF` cross (`×`) rather than a friendly curved checkmark.
